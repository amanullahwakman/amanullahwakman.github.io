Research

The Logic of Change and Continuity in Afghanistan's Neighbourhood Policy toward Iran

Comparative research examining changes and continuities in Afghanistan's neighbourhood policy toward Iran during the Republican period and the Second Islamic Emirate.

The research examines political-diplomatic, security, economic-commercial, cultural-social, identity-ideological, and environmental dimensions of neighbourhood policy.
