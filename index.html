About Me

I am Dr. Amanullah Wakman, a researcher in International Relations with academic and professional experience related to Afghanistan. My research focuses on foreign policy, international security, regional politics, Afghanistan's relations with neighbouring countries, and political developments in contemporary Afghanistan.

My academic interests also include neighbourhood policy, regional security, geopolitical change, political transformation, and foreign policy analysis.

Research

The Logic of Change and Continuity in Afghanistan's Neighbourhood Policy toward Iran
