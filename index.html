About Me

I am Amanullah Wakman, a researcher in International Relations specializing in Afghanistan's foreign policy, neighbourhood policy, regional politics, and political change and continuity.

My research focuses particularly on Afghanistan's relations with neighbouring and regional powers, including Iran, Pakistan, China, India, and Russia. I am also interested in peace and conflict, political stability and instability, soft power, governance, and major theoretical debates in International Relations.

My research agenda examines how states respond to changes in their regional and international environments and how political, economic, ideational, normative, and strategic factors shape foreign and neighbourhood policies.

Research

The Logic of Change and Continuity in Afghanistan's Neighbourhood Policy toward Iran

Comparative research examining changes and continuities in Afghanistan's neighbourhood policy toward Iran during the Republican period and the Second Islamic Emirate.

The research examines political-diplomatic, security, economic-commercial, cultural-social, identity-ideological, and environmental-natural resource dimensions of neighbourhood policy.

The Logic of Change and Continuity in Ukraine's Neighbourhood Policy toward Russia

Comparative research examining the evolution of Ukraine's neighbourhood policy toward Russia across different political periods and leaderships.
